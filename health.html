Your listening dose from Apple Health

iPhone and Apple Watch already measure how loud your headphones are and keep it in the Health app. This page reads that data from a file you export and shows how much of the WHO weekly safe-listening limit (80 dB(A) × 40 h) you've used.

Private: the file is read inside this browser tab. Nothing is uploaded, and nothing is stored.

1 · Export from Health

  1. Open the Health app and tap your profile picture (top right).
  2. Tap Export All Health Data and wait (it can take a minute or two).
  3. Choose Save to Files.

The export contains all your Health data. It never leaves your phone unless you share it.
