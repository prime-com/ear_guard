Set up

Which test?

On iPhone, opening the microphone changes Bluetooth headphone sound (milestone 1 result), so the hearing check is suggested here.

Before you start:

  • Turn on Do Not Disturb and close other apps that make sound — the volume will be at maximum.
  • Pick the headphones as the sound output in your system settings.
  • Headphones with wear detection pause when taken off your head. For the mic test, drape them over a pillow or ball so they stay awake.
  • Pick the computer's built-in microphone as the input (never the headphones' own mic).

Room check

Stay quiet for a few seconds while the microphone listens to the room.

…

Microphone test

  1. Set the computer's volume to maximum.
  2. Put the headphones off your head, on a table or pillow.
  3. Press one earcup/earbud flat against the computer's microphone (on most laptops: the tiny hole next to the camera or keyboard).
  4. Check: the sound comes from the headphones, not the laptop speakers.

Volume steps —

Keep the headphones against the mic. Now set the computer's volume to about of the maximum, then press the button.

Hearing check

  1. The microphone is now off.
  2. Set the volume back to maximum.
  3. Put the headphones on your head. Sit somewhere quiet.
  4. A very faint, pulsing beep will slowly get louder from silence. Press I hear it the moment you first hear it (or press the space bar). Don't guess — if you're unsure, wait.

Listen… press the button as soon as you hear the beep.
